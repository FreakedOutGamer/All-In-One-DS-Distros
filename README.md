# Pokémon All-In-One DS Distribution ROMs

Nintendo DS ROMs that send Pokémon Mystery Gift events over DS Wireless, the way
the official event cartridges did in stores. Each ROM holds a whole list of
events: pick one and send it to your game.

They are modifications of two real distribution cartridges,
**Winter 2011 Celebi** (Gen 4) and **Spring 2013 Meloetta** (Gen 5), with the
single event replaced by a menu of many.

> **These are free.** If you paid for them, or for a cartridge with them on it,
> you've been scammed.

## What's included

```
Gen 4/        Gen 4 Events (<language>).nds
Gen 5/        Gen 5 Events (<language>).nds
PBR/          PBR Items (<language>).nds
Unreleased/   Gen 4 Unreleased (<language>).nds
```

There is one ROM per language: ENG, FRE, GER, ITA, SPA, JPN and KOR. **Use the
ROM in the same language as your game**, or the game won't see the gift.
English ROMs work with US, UK and Australian games.

### PBR: `PBR Items`

The 49 item gifts from Pokémon Battle Revolution (Liechi Berry, Lansat Berry,
DeepSeaScale and so on), for Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold and
SoulSilver. Available in English, French, German, Italian, Spanish and
Japanese, with 49 gifts in each.

### Unreleased: `Gen 4 Unreleased`

21 gifts that exist in the Gen 4 games but were never distributed: the
Stopwatch, Alarm Clock and Azure Flute, seals, Underground ornaments, dolls, a
Manaphy Egg, the My Pokémon Ranch Pokémon and the Lock Capsule. Available in all
seven languages, with 21 gifts in each.

These Wonder Cards are **unofficial recreations**.

## What you need

- **Two systems**: one runs the distribution ROM, the other runs your Pokémon
  game. Both must be close to each other.
- **A way to run the ROM on real hardware**, such as a DS flashcart. The ROM
  has to send a real wireless signal, so **emulators can't send gifts**.

## How to use it

1. Start the distribution ROM. The bottom screen shows the selected gift:

   ```
   Gift 1 of 59
   001 - TRU Manaphy
   Games: Diamond/Pearl
   ```

   **Games** lists the games that can receive the gift. **All Games** means
   every game of that generation.

2. Choose a gift:
   - **Left / Right**: previous / next gift
   - **Up / Down**: jump 10 gifts forward / back
   - **L + R + Left / Right**: change the wireless channel (try this if your
     game can't find the gift)
3. Press **A** to start sending. Press **A** again to stop. You can only change
   the gift while it is stopped.
4. On your game, open **Mystery Gift** from the main menu and choose to receive
   a gift wirelessly. The game finds the gift and asks if you want it.

### Receiving in your game

- **Diamond, Pearl and Platinum**: Mystery Gift has to be unlocked first.
  Answer the questionnaire in the Jubilife TV building with: "EVERYONE HAPPY WI-FI CONNECTION". Afterwards, Mystery Gift appears on the main
  menu.
- **HeartGold and SoulSilver**: Mystery Gift is on the main menu after you've
  saved the game.
- **Black, White, Black 2 and White 2**: Mystery Gift is on the main menu from
  the start.
- In Gen 4, collect the gift from the **delivery person** in any Poké Mart.
  In Gen 5, collect it from the **delivery person** in any Pokémon Center.
- Your game can only hold a limited number of Wonder Cards. If it says it
  can't receive more, collect your pending gifts or delete an old card.

## Troubleshooting

- **The game doesn't find the gift.** Check that the ROM's language matches
  the game's. Check that the **Games** line includes your game. Make sure
  you've pressed A on the distribution ROM, and keep both systems close.
- **The game finds the gift but won't take it.** Some gifts can only be
  received once per save file or by version.
